# Motion Director

Read [direction.md](references/direction.md). Inspect the supplied content, not just its title. Establish what the viewer should understand and what proves it.

1. Write `brief.md`: single takeaway, audience/context, communication objective, intended feeling, format/duration/fps, copy language, CTA, asset requirements, deadline and unacceptable results. State assumptions; ask only about choices that would materially change the film.
2. Inventory assets in `assets.json` with id, local path, role, provenance, rights status, content approval, dimensions and required flag. Keep brand/product truth separate from creative choices. Never fabricate UI, results, reviews, logos or metrics. Permission to use a reference is not permission to reuse its music or imagery.
3. Inspect at least one provided reference when available. Write `reference-analysis.md`: measured palette, type classification, grid, focal hierarchy, meaningful change cadence, motion behavior, texture, audio relationship, traits to adapt and subjects/branding to exclude. Record file and sampled timestamps; label estimates. If there is no reference, state that and propose a concrete authored direction.
4. Propose two compact concepts with opening frame, visual mechanism and proof moment. Choose one against the brief in autonomous mode; preserve the selection reason. A concept is a relationship such as "friction becomes an uninterrupted path", not a list of effects.
5. Hand off a chosen concept, evidence-backed claims, required asset ids, style constraints and outstanding blockers to `motion-storyboard`. Create a mock styleframe for an uncertain design choice; do not spend a full render budget resolving it.

Treat G1 as complete only when required assets and truthful proof are available. Continue planning around missing assets without portraying the draft as production-ready.

## Showreel revisions

For a studio intro, brand reel or slideshow critique, resolve motion-studio by frontmatter name and read references/showreel-craft.md. Follow the motif/action/camera/reading plan and use executable craft tools when appropriate. Review encoded results; ambient wobble is not narrative motion and a machine pass is not creative approval.
