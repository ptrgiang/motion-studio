# Quality contract

Use motion-review's observed rubric when available. Maintain three distinct levels: machine structural validation, rendered visual/temporal inspection, and truthful final delivery. None substitutes for another.

## Minimum proof
- Same frame repeatedly and directly seekable in a controlled runtime.
- Actual source assets and no unsupported product claims.
- Every essential line readable at phone size during its visible hold.
- Contact sheets for each output format plus fast-transition strips.
- Rough/final video viewed for pacing and artifacts; required audio listened to and checked for sync.
- Actual encoded file matches fps, count, resolution, aspect ratio, requested streams and duration.
- Editable source, pinned dependencies and a tested rerender command preserved.

## Review cycles
Prioritize factual/corrupt output, then readability/continuity, then timing/mix, then ornament. Record three defects maximum per ordinary cycle and implement local fixes. Recheck neighboring shots and every affected format. Allow at most three aesthetic cycles by default. A cap limits effort, not honesty: incomplete checks or unresolved blockers remain draft.

## Delivery note
List files, observed checks, pending checks and reproduction command. Do not say "professional-grade", "fully reviewed" or "Opus generated this" without evidence. Explain that an AI skill enforces a workflow but cannot guarantee human aesthetic judgment or distribution performance.
